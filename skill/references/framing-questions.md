# Framing questions

Ask these before casting a section. They come from the Slideworks section
guides; the answers become storyline claims. Full method:
[docs/10-slide-method.md](../../docs/10-slide-method.md).

If a question has no sourced answer, the slide is either cut, marked
`inferred`, or the brief gets an `open_questions` row. Do not invent the
answer to fill a template.

## Every deck

- What is the **one question** this pack exists to answer?
- What do we want from the people in the room?
- Which metrics do they already believe?
- Who decides, and what do they need to see to say yes?

## Decision spine (business case)

**The deck:** What happens if we take this course of action?

| Section | Questions |
|---|---|
| Exec summary | What is the situation, the complication, the resolution, the number, the ask? Can this page stand alone? |
| Background | Why now? What is in and out of scope? Which strategic objective does this move? |
| Problem / opportunity | Can we say it in one sentence? What are the drivers? What does it cost us every week we wait? Who feels it? |
| Vision | What does better look like, qualitatively and in a number? Which processes or products change? |
| Options | What are the alternatives, including do-nothing? On which criteria (the room's, not ours) do they differ? |
| Recommendation | What exactly are they approving — scope, deliverables, what is out? Why this option? |
| Benefits | What is tangible? What is not? Where does each number come from? |
| Cost / ROI | Initial vs ongoing? In *their* ROI language (NPV, IRR, payback)? Where can cost overrun? |
| Implementation | When? Who? What is the first milestone? What does "yes" commit them to? |
| Risks | What could kill it? What is the mitigation, the owner, the date? |
| Governance | Who decides what, and how will we know it is working? |
| Next steps | What happens this week if they say yes? Who owns the ask? |

Pre-flight (brief, not slides): is the problem clear and short; does it move a
stated objective; have we brainstormed alternatives with the people who live
with them; do we have the data to compare; do we know the decider and how they
like to be sold?

## Playbook spine (GTM)

**New product:** How do we best launch and scale, across every function?
**Existing:** What do we focus on to find the next increment of growth?

| Section | Questions |
|---|---|
| Point of departure | What is already true? What objective is this GTM serving? |
| Markets | Which geographies and segments? Who are the spearheads / early evangelists? Which new geography is actually next? |
| Customers | Who buys, who influences? What is the pain, the current workaround, the journey? What are they measured on? |
| Product | Which features and value props does *this* segment actually pay for? How does the journey get better? |
| Competition | Who already offers this? To whom? What is uniquely ours — breakthrough, or just better? Is there demand or a pile-up? |
| Pricing | How do we capture what the market will bear and still clear cost? Strategy vs model vs tactics? |
| Sales | Self-serve, inside, field, or channel? Why that motion for this product and buyer? How does a lead become cash? |
| Distribution | Where do we meet them for conversion? Direct, partner, platform — why? |
| Marketing | Which vehicles does this buyer already attend to? How does that map to awareness / consideration / decision? |
| Customer success | How do we implement, retain, and expand? What is the upsell / cross-sell motion? |
| Plan | What can be assigned a status and started tomorrow? Which KPIs will the next meeting score? |

Lockstep test: if marketing, sales, and success could each run this pack
without talking, the GTM is not a GTM.

## Weekly / board

- What changed since last time?
- What is the one decision or resource we need now?
- What did we say we would do, and did we?
- What is inferred vs measured?

Do not invent a third spine. Borrow SCR for a decision, the playbook for a
motion, and keep the pack short.
